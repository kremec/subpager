When a street or settlement with a house number is supplied, prefer that postal address. Include its FULL house number with every letter or suffix and directly adjacent municipality/settlement context. A named institution or identifying acronym directly next to the address may also be included. Start at that name or the address itself. Exclude the preceding incident description, machinery, vehicles, casualties, generic rooms or floors, and approach descriptions such as near a bridge or before an address. If unwanted words separate the site name from the address, select the contiguous address alone rather than copying those words. When no numbered address is supplied, keep the named school, building, landmark or settlement that identifies the destination. For a destination described by its position relative to a landmark, preserve the spatial relationship and its prepositions. Do not drop a useful named place merely because it has no street or house number.
